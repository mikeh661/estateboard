Add someone

Creates their login. Tell them their email and the temporary password; they can change it with "Forgotten your password?" on the sign-in screen.

When someone leaves

Set their access to No access. They're locked out straight away, on every device, and their name stays on any changes they made.

The TV should have its own View only login, so a leaver's password is never the one the TV uses.

Agency

How the office display looks. Changes show on the board straight away.

Sales this year

Shown on the board as "sales this year" (1 April to 31 March). Enter the sales completed this year before the board went live. Every sale moved to Sold on the board is added on automatically, and the count starts again on 1 April.

Backup
